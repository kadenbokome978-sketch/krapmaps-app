## ⭐ LOGGED — The Brand-Deal Marketplace (flagship platform play)

**Rating: 9/10 ceiling · ~6/10 near-term (cold-start dependent)**

### The idea
A two-sided marketplace connecting **brands** with **creators**, where our AI does the matching using **real performance data we already have** (virality scores, actual outcomes, Voice DNA, niche fit) — data brands and agencies genuinely can't get anywhere else.

- **Brands** post a campaign brief (budget, niche, deliverable, goals).
- **Our AI matches** the best-fit creators — not by follower count (vanity), but by *predicted performance for that brief*, grounded in the corpus + each creator's real outcomes.
- **We take 10–15%** of every deal closed through the platform.

### Why it's revolutionary
- **Transaction revenue, not SaaS** — uncapped upside; revenue scales with deal volume, not seat count.
- **Data moat** — the matching quality comes from the cross-creator corpus + real outcome data. A competitor with a prettier UI can't replicate the *matching intelligence*.
- **Aligns everyone** — creators get relevant deals, brands get creators who'll actually perform, we earn on outcomes.
- It turns a £29/mo tool into a **platform** with a defensible network effect (more creators → better matches → more brands → more creators).

### What it reuses (already built)
- The **Deals** tab (deal tracking, statuses, pipeline).
- The **scoring engine** + **cross-creator corpus** (the matching brain).
- **Voice DNA** + real posted outcomes per creator (the "will they perform" signal).
- The **audit/prospecting** tech (analyzing any creator's real numbers).

### What's new / needed
- Brand-side onboarding + campaign brief flow.
- A matching algorithm (rank creators by predicted fit/performance for a brief).
- Payments/escrow + our cut (Stripe Connect or similar).
- Trust/ratings layer (completed deals, delivery reliability).

### The hard part (be honest)
- **Two-sided cold start** — need creator supply AND brand demand simultaneously. This is why it's a **Phase 3–4 play**, not now.
- **Bootstrapping route:** seed the creator side from the existing user base + DFY/agency clients first; then bring in brands once there's enough quality supply to match against. Don't launch the marketplace cold — grow into it from the agency/creator base.

### Where it sits in the plan
Phase 4 (Months 6–12). Only viable once we have a dense creator base (from Phase 1–3: DFY clients → self-serve creators → agency accounts). The agency + creator base becomes the marketplace's initial supply.

## The full ranked idea list (for context)

1. **Sell to agencies, not solo creators** — 9.5/10. GTM pivot; 10x revenue/sale, kills churn. Same product, white-labelled.
2. **Become your own agency first** — 9/10. Sell the outcome; 3–5 DFY clients at £1–3k/mo = cash + proof now.
3. **Brand-deal marketplace** — 9/10 ceiling (LOGGED above).
4. **Free viral "score my last video" tool** — 8.5/10. Public, shareable lead-gen engine. Attacks distribution gap.
5. **Niche down, own one vertical** — 8.5/10. Dense corpus + sharp marketing in one niche.
6. **Scoring-as-an-API / license the engine** — 8/10. B2B recurring, near-zero marginal cost.
7. **Corpus as a "Virality Intelligence" data product** — 7.5/10. Sell niche performance reports to brands/agencies.
8. **Competitor-teardown paid wedge** — 7.5/10. "Spy on any creator, get their formula."
9. **Faceless / AI video generation tier** — 7/10. Big TAM, real API cost. Phase 3.
10. **Outcome-based pricing** — 6.5/10. Charge on results; only after scoring is proven.

## The plan (phased — every phase must produce revenue, proof, or distribution)

- **Phase 0 (wk 1–2) — Unblock:** TikTok/IG alignment (honest labelled fix), billing live (£19 founding tier), rotate IG secret.
- **Phase 1 (wk 2–6) — Proof & cash:** run KrapMaps through the tool + log outcomes (proof); land 3 DFY clients at £750–1,500/mo. Target £3–5k MRR from services.
- **Phase 2 (wk 6–12) — Package & distribute:** free "Score My Video" tool; launch self-serve with proof (£29 / £79); own one niche. Target 20 self-serve + 5 DFY, ~£8–10k MRR.
- **Phase 3 (mo 4–6) — Scale:** agency tier (£499+/mo); guarantee tier; corpus data reports. Target £20k+ MRR.
- **Phase 4 (mo 6–12) — Platform:** Scoring API; seed the **marketplace** from the agency/creator base.

**Rule:** no new feature unless it serves the current phase's money goal. The real bottleneck is proof + distribution, not features.
