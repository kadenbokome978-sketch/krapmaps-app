# Greenlit — In-Depth Business Plan & Monetization Map (2026)

*Honest, bottom-up. Every figure states its assumption. No hype numbers.*

---

## 0. One-line thesis

Greenlit predicts whether a short-form video will hit **before it's filmed**, grounded in the creator's *own* results and written in their *own* voice — and it compounds: every post makes it sharper and every audit fattens a cross-creator data moat competitors can't clone.

**Current honest state:** the product is over-built for its customer count (zero paying, today). The engine is genuinely strong. The bottleneck is **proof + distribution**, not features. This plan is built around fixing that, then scaling.

## 2. The model — bottom-up, three paths

Assumptions stated per line. Costs: Opus 4.8 with prompt caching ≈ **$0.03–0.08 per audit**, Bright Data scrape ≈ **$0.045 per creator** (30 videos). So a free audit costs you **~$0.10 all-in**. This is the cheapest customer-acquisition asset you have.

### Path A — Done-for-you "Growth Sprint" (start here)

The fastest cash and the fastest proof. You run the engine *for* the creator, 30 days, high-touch.

| Metric | Conservative | Base | Aggressive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sprint price | £297 | £297 | £497 |
| Sprints / month (by month 3) | 2 | 4 | 6 |
| Monthly revenue (m3) | £594 | £1,188 | £2,982 |
| Cost to deliver (your time + ~£5 compute) | low | low | low |
| **Run-rate by month 6** | £1.2k/mo | £3–4k/mo | £8k+/mo |

Why start here: **£297 × 4 = £1,188/mo** from *four* clients is life-changing margin for a solo operator, and each Sprint **manufactures the case study** Path B needs. This is the bootstrap engine.

### Path B — Self-serve SaaS (the scalable layer)

Only turns on *after* you have 2–3 case studies from Path A.

| Metric | Conservative | Base | Aggressive |
|---|---|---|---|
| Pro price | £29/mo | £29/mo | £29/mo |
| Free→Pro conversion | 3% | 6% | 10% |
| Paying subs by month 12 | 40 | 150 | 400 |
| MRR month 12 | £1,160 | £4,350 | £11,600 |
| Gross margin | ~92% | ~92% | ~92% |
| **ARR month 12** | £14k | £52k | £139k |

Churn assumption: 6–8% monthly early (high for prosumer), improving to 4% as the "gets smarter the longer you use it" lock-in kicks in. The compounding data moat is the retention story — lean into it.

### Path C — Marketplace / brand-deal cut (the upside option)

Match creators to brand deals, take a cut. High ceiling, high effort, **do not build until Path A+B are humming** — it's a different business (two-sided marketplace) with its own cold-start.

| Metric | If it takes off (year 2–3) |
|---|---|
| Deals brokered / month | 30 |
| Avg deal value | £1,500 |
| Your cut | 15% |
| Monthly revenue | £6,750 |
| Blended with SaaS | could 2–3× total revenue |

Rating of Path C: **high reward (8/10), high risk (7/10), wrong timing now.** Park it. Revisit at £10k MRR.

## 3. 12-month sequenced roadmap

**Months 0–1 — Prove it.** Send 30 free audits to finance creators (cost: ~£3). Convert 2–3 to a £297 Sprint. Goal: **first £600–900 + first testimonial.**

**Months 2–3 — Systematize the Sprint.** Templatize delivery. Raise to 4 Sprints/mo. Publish 2 case studies (before/after view counts). Goal: **£1k+/mo, 2 case studies.**

**Months 4–6 — Turn on self-serve.** Point the case studies at the landing page. Open Pro (£29). Seed 3 more niches into the corpus. Goal: **£3–4k/mo blended.**

**Months 7–12 — Scale the funnel.** Paid content (you posting audits as content is free distribution), affiliate/referral loop, one lightweight integration (Later/Metricool). Goal: **£5–12k MRR.**

**Year 2 — Optionality.** If SaaS retention is strong → double down on product. If you're closing brand deals by hand anyway → build Path C.

## 5. Where the business can go — future monetization (ranked by leverage × feasibility)

1. **Niche corpus packs (high leverage, low effort).** Seed the top 40 creators in each of 6–8 niches. Each seeded niche makes audits in it visibly sharper → higher close rate → *and* becomes a marketing claim ("the only tool trained on the top 40 finance creators"). Compounds for free from every audit run. **Do this next.**

2. **"Done-with-you" tier (£99/mo) between Pro and Sprint.** Captures creators who want more than software but can't afford £297. Fills the pricing gap. High margin.

3. **Agency / white-label seats.** Social-media agencies managing 10+ creators would pay £199–499/mo for multi-account. The whitelabel config already exists (client.config.json). **Reuses built infrastructure.**

4. **Content-as-distribution flywheel.** You posting real audits ("I scored this creator's next video — here's what the AI said") *is* the marketing. Free, on-brand, proves the product publicly. Zero ad spend.

5. **Referral loop.** Every audit ends with "want your friend audited? send this link." Viral coefficient on a free, valuable, shareable asset.

6. **Brand-deal marketplace (Path C).** The big swing. Year 2+.

7. **API / embed.** Let other creator tools call your scoring engine. Long-term platform play.
