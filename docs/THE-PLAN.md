## The core call (read this first)

You are NOT going to get rich selling a $29/mo app to solo creators. The research is brutal
and clear on this:
- $29 is the *impulse ceiling* for creator tools; churn is high; CAC is brutal.
- "Predict my video" is already a ~$300M-ARR commodity (Higgsfield) — you can't win there.

So the app is **not the product you sell — it's the weapon you sell WITH.** The money comes from:
1. **You running it for clients (DFY services)** — fast cash + proof. *This month.*
2. **Licensing it to agencies white-label** — 10x ARPU, validated market. *Once you have proof.*
3. **The free audit** — your entire top-of-funnel and the answer to "I have no followers."

Everything below serves that. If a feature or task doesn't help you land or keep a *paying*
client in the next 90 days, it waits.

## 1. WHO you sell to — pick ONE niche, and pick this one

**Target niche: finance / "make money online" / business creators (10k–150k followers).**

Why this niche specifically (research-backed):
- **They have money.** Finance/MMO/business is the **highest-CPM vertical ($15–22 CPM)** — these
  creators earn the most per view and are the most comfortable *paying for ROI*. A fitness
  creator haggles over £50; a finance creator spending £750 to make £3k doesn't blink.
- **They think in ROI**, so your pitch ("I'll grow your views → more clients/affiliate/deals")
  lands in a language they already speak.
- **They're used to buying tools and courses** — low friction to a services sale.
- **Dense corpus compounds:** every audit and client in one niche makes your predictions and
  audits sharper *in that niche*, which makes the next sale easier. Spreading across niches
  kills this.

If finance isn't your world, second choice: **digital-marketing / agency-owner creators**
(also $12–18 CPM, also ROI buyers). Do NOT pick lifestyle/comedy — high effort, low budgets.

**Buyer #1 (now): the stuck sub-150k finance creator** — plateaued, posts consistently, scared
of the algorithm, has income but no system.
**Buyer #2 (scale): small agencies / SMMs** serving those creators — white-label.
**Buyer #3 (later): brands** doing UGC ads in that vertical — pre-testing creative (highest £/customer).

## 2. HOW you reach them — the exact funnel + the real numbers

**The free audit is the outreach.** You never cold-pitch "buy my tool." You send a creator a
teardown of *their own* content that's better than anything they've paid for. The audit's
quality is your credential — nobody checks your follower count after that.

**The math (from research — use it, don't guess):**
- Generic outreach: ~1% response. **Personalized, value-first outreach: ~10.5% response.**
- It takes **5 touches** to engage someone; **follow-ups spaced 2–5 days lift conversion 49%.**
- Multi-channel (DM + email + comment) beats single-channel by 287%.

**Realistic conversion funnel to your first 3 clients:**
```
150 personalized free audits sent (over ~4 weeks, one niche)
  → ~15–25 replies        (value-first = 10–15% reply)
  → ~5–8 booked calls      (you led with a real teardown)
  → ~2–3 paying DFY clients (£750–1,500/mo each)
```
That's **~30–40 audits/week.** It's grindy. It's also the whole game. Do it.

**Where to find them:** the "similar creators" rail off the niche's big accounts, niche
hashtags, comment sections of the top 10 finance creators, finance Discords/subreddits,
and LinkedIn for the agency tier. Personalized connection notes get **30%+ acceptance.**

**The message shape (value-first, one specific signal):**
> "Ran your last 30 videos through my system — 60% are losing people at the 3-second mark,
> and your best-performing format (X) you've only used twice. Full breakdown attached [audit].
> Want me to fix the hook pattern for you?"

Then follow up **twice**, 3 days apart. Most sales are in the follow-up.

## 3. WHAT to build — my real feature recommendations (decisive)

I'm not giving you a menu. Build these **four**, in this order, because each one directly
makes you money or keeps a client. Everything else on the idea lists waits.

### BUILD #1 — The Free Audit → shareable PDF/link (THE growth engine) ⭐⭐
Upgrade the existing audit into a **screenshot-worthy, watermarked, one-click PDF/share-link**:
hook analysis, retention drop-off, their best/worst format, 3 specific fixes, predicted upside.
This is your entire top-of-funnel. Every audit sent is an ad + a sales opener. **Highest ROI
thing in the codebase.** Uses public/scraped data, so the organic-views issue never blocks it.
→ **Priority: build first. This is what makes money.**

### BUILD #2 — The Proof Engine (F1) ⭐⭐
Auto-log each video's *prediction* at post time; reconcile vs *actual* a week later; show a
running "within X% on your last N videos" scorecard per creator.
Why it matters: it's the **one moat Higgsfield can't copy** (they never see your outcomes), it
turns "AI can't predict exact numbers" into a *trust asset*, and it manufactures your case
studies automatically ("grew this client 40% — here's the receipt"). Case studies are the gate
to the agency tier.
→ **Priority: build second. This is what proves you.**

### BUILD #3 — Media Kit + Rate Calculator (F3) ⭐
One click → brand-ready media kit from real synced numbers + "what to charge" calculator.
Creators *desperately* want this; it's a reason to keep the app open; watermarked = more
distribution; and it's the on-ramp to the eventual marketplace.
→ **Priority: build third. This is what retains + spreads.**

### BUILD #4 — Money Dashboard (F2) ⭐
Reframe the home view around **£, not views**: brand deals + creator fund + predicted earnings
in one "you're on track for £X this month" view.
Why: people renew a tool that shows them money, not vanity metrics. Cheap (reuses Deals +
scoring). Biggest retention lever for the paid tier.
→ **Priority: build fourth. This is what keeps them paying.**

**Do NOT** pour effort into: beating Higgsfield on raw prediction, the cadence optimizer (F8),
or the marketplace (too early). The Video Checker is *table stakes* — keep it working, don't
gold-plate it.

## 4. THE 90-DAY EXECUTION PLAN (week by week, with £ targets)

### Phase 0 — Weeks 1–2 · Arm the funnel (target: £0, fully loaded)
- **Build #1 (Audit→PDF)** and **Build #2 (Proof Engine logging)**.
- Rotate the leaked IG secret. Get **billing live** (£19 founding / £29 standard).
- Write the outreach message + a 2-step follow-up sequence.
- Pick the niche (finance/MMO). Build a list of **150 target creators**.

### Phase 1 — Weeks 2–6 · First cash + proof (target: £2–4k, 2–3 clients)
- Run **you + Thierno** through the tool; log predicted-vs-actual (feeds Proof Engine + your
  own case study).
- Send **30–40 personalized free audits/week** into the niche. Follow up twice.
- Convert **2–3 into DFY clients at £750–1,500/mo.** ← your first real money.
- Deliver hard for them; screenshot every win.

### Phase 2 — Weeks 6–12 · Package + start scaling (target: £5–8k MRR)
- Turn client wins into **2–3 case studies** (the Proof Engine makes these for you).
- **Build #3 (Media Kit)** + **Build #4 (Money Dashboard)**.
- Launch **self-serve (£19/£29)** with the free audit as the funnel — funnel, not main income.
- Open **agency (white-label) conversations** using the case studies. LinkedIn + agency groups.

### Phase 3 — Months 4–6 · High-ARPU engine (target: £15–25k MRR)
- Land **2–3 white-label agency deals** (£300–800/mo each — one = ~20 creator subs).
- Open **1–2 brand/UGC-ad pre-testing** conversations (one deal beats 50 subs).
- Free-audit loop runs always-on as distribution.

### Phase 4 — Months 6–12 · Platform (target: £40k+ MRR)
- Seed the **brand-deal marketplace** from the agency + creator base (never cold).
- License the **prediction API** if a buyer appears.
