Everything else is noise. **12 personalised audits sent per day, each followed up twice.** Do that for ~2 weeks and the math below lands you a client. Miss days and it doesn't. That's the whole game.

## THE OFFER (the single most important decision)

**Do NOT lead with a monthly retainer.** First-time buyers won't commit to £X/month for someone they just met. They buy **low-commitment, fixed-scope, time-boxed** things.

### 🎯 "The 30-Day Greenlit Sprint" — one-off
**Founding rate: £250–500** (you have zero case studies yet — price for the YES, not the ceiling. Raise it the moment you have one result to point to.)

**What they get (all powered by Greenlit, so it's low-effort for you):**
- Full **channel audit + content strategy** (what's working, what's costing them views, their Voice DNA).
- **20 video ideas scored *before* they film** — so they stop wasting posts.
- A **30-day posting plan** (what to post, when).
- **The Video Checker**: they send a clip before posting, you give GO / RISKY / NO.
- **Weekly 15-min check-in** + a predicted-vs-actual tracking sheet.

**Why it closes:** one-off · 30 days · fixed price · risk-reversed = the easy first yes.

**The risk-reversal (kills "will it work?"):**
> "If in 30 days your typical views haven't moved, I refund you. You risk nothing but the time to read the plan."

**The back-end (later money):** after a successful sprint, roll them to **£300–750/month** to keep it running. Sprint = the first yes. Retainer = the compounding income. For £1k you only need the sprint.

## PHASE 1 — Days 3–14: The outreach engine (this IS the job)

Same rhythm every day. ~45–75 min. Do it on the train, whenever.
1. **Send 12 fresh audits** — run the handle → screenshot the sharpest finding → DM. Log each.
2. **Follow up** everyone from **3 days ago** (FU1) and **6 days ago** (FU2). *>50% of replies come from follow-ups. Never skip this.*
3. **Reply to responders** → get them on a call (send Calendly).
4. **Refill the list** when low (+20 handles).

**Cadence per prospect:** Day 0 audit → Day 3 "here's the breakdown anyway 👇" → Day 6 "the hook fix is the one I'd start with" → then move on.

**Checkpoints (be honest):**
- **End Week 1:** ~60 sent, first replies. If **0 replies from 40+ → your DM/targeting is off.** Make the opener more specific (one real detail from their content) before sending more.
- **End Week 2:** ~120 sent, **3–6 replies, 1–3 calls.** On track.

---

## PHASE 2 — Days 8–21: Close

Calls land around Week 2. Keep them 15 min, tight:
1. **Their goal** (2m): "What are you trying to hit — followers, or more sales from your [course/link]?"
2. **Walk the audit** (5m): show 2–3 sharpest findings. *Let the audit sell — they saw you get their content.*
3. **The gap** (2m): "You're sitting on [their best format] and barely using it. Your hooks lose 60% in 3 seconds. That's fixable."
4. **Present the Sprint** (3m): 30 days, the price, the risk-reversal.
5. **Close** (3m): "Want to start Monday?" → silence → send the Stripe link **on the call**. Get the card while they're hot.

**Objections:**
- *"Too expensive"* → "Less than one brand deal, and if your views don't move you're refunded — real risk is £0."
- *"Let me think"* → "Totally — what's the one thing you're unsure about?" (answer the real objection) → "I take 2 sprint clients a month, want me to hold your spot?"
- *"How do I know it works?"* → risk-reversal + "I run my own + a mate's channel through the exact same system." (Never fake clients — the refund carries it.)

**Deliver like your reputation depends on it (it does):** run their sprint *through Greenlit* (dogfood it), over-communicate, screenshot every win. Client #1 becomes the case study that makes #2 ten times easier.
