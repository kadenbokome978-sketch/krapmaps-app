# Road to £1,000 — the no-BS, day-by-day plan

> Goal: first **£1,000 of real revenue**, the startup milestone that proves this is a business, not a project.
> Grounded in market research. Realistic timeline: **£1k by end of Week 3–4** if you execute daily. Could slip to
> Week 5–6 if reply rates run low — the fix is always *more volume + more follow-up*, never "wait." No fluff below.

---

## The core logic (why this works, in numbers)

Research on cold outreach for new freelancers/agencies (2026):
- **First client lands in 2–8 weeks**; the fastest do it **inside a month** by sending **5+ personalised messages/day**.
- A good funnel: **~16% reply rate**, **~60% of replies → a call**, **~50–60% of calls → close** (with a strong, low-risk offer).
- **>50% of replies come from FOLLOW-UPS**, not the first message. Follow-up is not optional — it's where the money is.
- A one-off **"audit + strategy" deliverable prices at £1,500–£5,000** in the market. So a **£997 sprint is a *bargain*** — that gap is your unfair close.

**The math to £1k (conservative, realistic):**
```
~120 free audits sent (≈12/day for 10 days, each followed up twice)
  → ~15–20 replies        (12–16% — your value-first audit beats generic cold DMs)
  → ~5–7 booked calls
  → ~1–2 closes @ £997     = £1,000–£2,000
```
So: **one sale of the offer below = your £1k.** That's the whole game. Everything here serves getting ~120 sharp audits out and following up like your rent depends on it.

## THE OFFER (this is the single most important decision)

**Do NOT lead with "£750/month."** Research is blunt: retainers need a **3–6 month commitment**, which is a *huge* ask for a first client who's never heard of you. First-timers buy **low-commitment, fixed-scope, time-boxed** things. So:

### 🎯 "The 30-Day Growth Sprint" — **£997** (one-off)
*(Price the very first one at £750 as a "founding client" rate if you need the momentum — either hits ~£1k.)*

**What they get (all powered by CreatorOS, so it's low-effort for you):**
- A full **content strategy** for their page (niche + Voice DNA + what's working/what's costing views).
- **20 video ideas scored *before* they film** — so they stop wasting posts.
- A **30-day posting plan** (what to post, when).
- **Weekly check-in** (15 min) + a tracking sheet of predicted vs actual.
- **The Video Checker**: they send you a clip before posting, you give GO/RISKY/NO.

**Why this closes:**
- **One-off, 30 days, fixed price** = no scary commitment → first-timers say yes.
- **£997 vs the £1,500–5,000 market rate** for audit+strategy = obvious value.
- **Risk-reversal** (below) removes the last objection.
- It **hits £1k in a single sale.**

**The risk-reversal (kills the "will it work?" objection):**
> "If in 30 days your typical views haven't moved, I refund you. You risk nothing but the time to read the plan."

**The back-end (where the real money is later):** after a successful sprint, roll them onto **£750/month DFY** to *keep* it going. Sprint = the easy first yes; retainer = the compounding income. But for £1k, you only need the sprint.

## PHASE 0 — Days 1–2: Build the machine (NO outreach yet)

Resist sending anything until the machine is built. Two days, then you're a factory.

### Day 1 — Offer, assets, rails
- [ ] **Lock the offer & price** (Sprint £997 / £750 founding). Write it as a **one-page Google Doc** — the 5 bullets above + the risk-reversal. This is what you send when they ask "what do you do?"
- [ ] **Set up a Stripe Payment Link** for £997 (Stripe dashboard → Payment Links → 2 mins). This is how you collect — send the link, they pay, done. No invoicing faff.
- [ ] **Set up a free Calendly** (15-min "Content Teardown Call"). One link to book calls.
- [ ] **Prep your personal TikTok** — you don't need a business bio, just make sure it looks like a real human (it does).
- [ ] **Build a tracking sheet** (Google Sheets): columns = Handle · Niche fit · Audit sent (date) · Replied? · FU1 · FU2 · Call booked · Outcome. *This sheet is your business.*
- [ ] **Save the DM + 2 follow-ups** (from `docs/OUTREACH.md`) somewhere you can paste fast.

### Day 2 — The list + dry run
- [ ] **Build a list of 60 target creators** (finance / make-money / side-hustle, **20k–100k**, **monetising** [course/link in bio], **plateaued** [flat or declining views]). Method: find one, tap the "suggested accounts" rail, save 60 handles into the sheet. ~45 mins.
- [ ] **Run your first 10 audits** through the app → confirm the scraper + audit output look clean and screenshot-worthy. Fix anything that looks off *now*, before you send at volume.
- [ ] **Send those first 10** with the DM. (Yes, start on Day 2 — momentum.)

**End of Phase 0:** offer locked, payment link live, 60 targets loaded, 10 audits out.

## PHASE 1 — Days 3–14: The outreach engine (the grind that makes the money)

This is 90% of the work and 100% of the result. Same rhythm every day. ~45–75 mins/day.

**Daily routine (do it on the train, whenever):**
1. **Send 12 fresh audits** — run the handle → screenshot → DM. Log each in the sheet.
2. **Follow up** on everyone from **3 days ago** who didn't reply (FU1) and **6 days ago** (FU2). *This is where >50% of replies come from — never skip it.*
3. **Reply to anyone who responded** → get them on a call (send Calendly).
4. **Refill the list** when it's running low (another 20 handles).

**Daily target:** 12 sent + all follow-ups done. Over 10 active days ≈ **120 audits sent**, each hit up to 3× = ~360 touches. That's your funnel.

**Cadence per prospect** (research-backed — most replies are on touch 2–3):
- Day 0: audit + opener.
- Day 3: FU1 ("here's the breakdown anyway 👇").
- Day 6: FU2 (soft: "the hook fix is the one I'd start with").
- After that, leave them — move on.

**Weekly checkpoints (be honest with yourself):**
- **End Week 1:** ~60 audits sent, first few replies trickling in. If **0 replies from 40+ sent → your DM or targeting is off.** Fix the opener (more specific, one real detail from their content) before sending more.
- **End Week 2:** ~120 sent, **3–6 replies, 1–3 calls booked.** On track.

## PHASE 2 — Days 8–21: Close the deal

Calls start landing around Week 2. This is where £1k gets collected.

### The call (15 mins, keep it tight — script):
1. **Rapport + their goal** (2 min): "What are you trying to hit — more followers, more sales from your [course/link]?"
2. **Walk the audit** (5 min): show them the 2–3 sharpest findings. *Let the audit do the selling* — they already saw you get their content.
3. **The gap** (2 min): "You're sitting on [their best format] and barely using it. Your hooks are losing 60% in 3 seconds. That's fixable."
4. **Present the Sprint** (3 min): the 30-day offer, £997, the risk-reversal. "I run your whole content engine for 30 days — plan, score every idea before you film, track what hits. If your views don't move, you walk, no charge."
5. **Close** (3 min): "Want to start Monday?" → silence → send the **Stripe link** on the call. *Get the card while they're hot.*

### Objection handling (the 3 you'll hear):
- **"Too expensive"** → "It's less than one brand deal, and the market rate for this is £2–5k. Plus if it doesn't move your views, you're refunded — so the real risk is £0."
- **"Let me think about it"** → "Totally — what's the one thing you're unsure about?" (surface the *real* objection, answer it). Then: "I only take 2 sprint clients a month, want me to hold your spot?"
- **"How do I know it works?"** → the risk-reversal + "I'm running my own + a mate's through the exact same system." (Never claim clients you don't have — the refund promise carries it.)

### Deliver like your reputation depends on it (because it does):
- Run their sprint **through CreatorOS** (dogfood it — proves the tool).
- Over-communicate. Screenshot every win.
- This first client becomes your **case study** → client #2 is 10× easier.

## The 3 rules that decide whether you hit £1k

1. **Send every day.** 12 audits, no zero days. Consistency beats intensity — a burst then silence kills the funnel.
2. **Always follow up.** Half your money is in touch 2 and 3. The founders who quit after one message are the ones who "cold outreach doesn't work for."
3. **Never fake proof.** No invented client results. Land #1 honestly on the risk-reversal → *that* real result is the proof that makes #2–#10 easy.

## Honest reality check (the no-BS part)

- **£1k in 3 weeks is realistic but not guaranteed.** Research says first client = 2–8 weeks. If you hit Week 4 with no close, you're not failing — you're in the normal range. Keep sending.
- **The #1 failure mode is stopping.** Not the product, not the price — people send 20 DMs, get no instant reply, and quit *right before* the follow-up replies land. Don't be that person.
- **Your product is ready. Your scraper works. Your niche and scripts are locked.** There is nothing left to build. The only variable now is *how many audits you send and follow up on.*

**Day 1 starts with one action: lock the offer and set up the Stripe link.** Everything flows from there.
