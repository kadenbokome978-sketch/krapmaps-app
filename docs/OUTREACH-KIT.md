# Greenlit — Finance Outreach Kit

Everything you need to send your first 30 audits and land your first Growth Sprint. The audit does the selling; your job is just to get it in front of the right people and follow up.

> **On the creator list below:** these are real, well-known money/finance creators, but handles drift (rebrands, platform moves). **Verify each handle before sending** — search the name on TikTok/IG and confirm. Treat the list as targeting, the *scripts* as the durable asset.

---

## 1. Who to target (the sweet spot)

Not the mega-creators (they have teams and won't reply). Not sub-1k (no budget). Target the **middle**:

- **20k–250k followers** — big enough to have money and take it seriously, small enough to read their own DMs.
- **Posts consistently** (2+ / week) — they care about growth, so a growth tool lands.
- **Visible inconsistency** — some videos pop, most don't. That gap is exactly what the audit exposes and what makes them feel seen.
- **Sells something** (course, newsletter, coaching, affiliate) — they have revenue, so £297 is a rounding error if you help them grow.

## 3. The send sequence

**Step 1 — Run the audit first.** Before you DM, run their handle through the Greenlit audit. Now you have a real, specific, flattering-but-honest breakdown in hand. *Never* DM cold without it.

**Step 2 — The opener DM** (short, specific, no pitch):

> Hey [name] — I ran your last ~30 videos through an AI that predicts which ones will hit before you post. One thing jumped out: your typical video does [median] views but you've already hit [ceiling] — that's [X]× your normal. I mapped exactly what's different about the ones that pop. Want me to send it over? No charge, no catch — just think your stuff deserves more reach.

**Step 3 — Deliver the audit** (when they say yes): send the shareable audit image/report. Let it speak. Add one line:

> Here you go. The 5 ideas at the bottom are scored on how likely they are to beat your average — the engine only surfaces ones it'd stake 81+ on. Steal any of them.

**Step 4 — The soft Sprint offer** (only after they've reacted well):

> Glad it's useful. If you ever want this run *for* you every week — every idea scored before you film, a 30-day plan, and me tracking what hits — that's the thing I actually do. First few founding spots are £297 for the month. No pressure either way.

## 4. Follow-up rules

- **No reply in 3 days →** one bump: *"No worries if not — want me to just send the audit over anyway? It's done."* (Sending the free thing unprompted often restarts the convo.)
- **Positive but no Sprint →** stay in touch. Like their videos, reply occasionally. Re-offer in 2–3 weeks with a fresh angle ("just added [feature] — thought of your channel").
- **Track everything** in a simple sheet: handle, follower count, audit sent (y/n), reply, status. 30 sends → expect ~10 replies → ~3 audits delivered → ~1 Sprint. That's your first £297 and your first case study.

## 5. Turning the first client into the flywheel

The moment one creator's video (that Greenlit scored high) actually pops:
- Screenshot the prediction vs the result. **That's your first case study** — put it on the landing page.
- Ask for one line of testimonial.
- Post it yourself as content: *"I predicted this creator's video would hit before they posted. Here's what happened."* Free distribution, proves the product publicly, pulls in the next leads.

**One closed Sprint funds months of Bright Data + AI costs and gives you the proof that turns the self-serve funnel on. The whole business unlocks from the first 30 sends.**
