## 1. Who to target (matters more than follower count)

Two filters beat any follower number:
1. **They monetise** — course, coaching, product, link in bio → money + a reason to grow.
2. **They're plateaued** — flat or declining views → the pain you solve.

**Money + stuck = ripe.** Sweet spot: **20k–100k followers.**
- <15k: usually can't comfortably pay — send the free audit for reputation, don't chase.
- 20k–100k: earning, still read their DMs, no agency yet, flattered you reached out. **← spend your energy here.**
- 150k+: often have management, ignore cold DMs.

## 2. Find 20 targets in 20 minutes
1. TikTok search: **"make money online" · "personal finance" · "side hustle" · "faceless finance" · "budgeting tips"**.
2. Find one creator at 20k–100k who monetises.
3. Open profile → tap the **suggested-accounts arrow** → that rail is 20 more just like them.
4. Save handles to your tracker. Skip anyone clearly faking it or already huge.

## 3. The daily loop (45–75 min)
1. **Send 12 fresh audits** — run handle in Greenlit → save the share-image → DM the opener.
2. **Follow up** everyone from **3 days ago** (FU1) and **6 days ago** (FU2).
3. **Reply to responders** → send your Calendly.
4. **Refill** the list when low (+20 handles).

Target: **12/day × 10 active days ≈ 120 audits → 1–2 closes = your first £1k.**

## 6. The call (15 min — book via Calendly, keep it tight)
1. **Their goal** (2m): "What are you trying to hit — more followers, or more sales from your [course/link]?"
2. **Walk the audit** (5m): show the 2–3 sharpest findings. *Let the audit sell — they already saw you get their content.*
3. **The gap** (2m): "You're sitting on [their best format] and barely using it. Hooks lose 60% in 3s. That's fixable."
4. **Present the sprint** (3m): 30 days, £297, the refund.
5. **Close** (3m): "Want to start Monday?" → **stay silent** → send the **Stripe link on the call.** Get the card while they're hot.

### Objections
- **"Too expensive"** → "Less than one brand deal — and if your views don't move you're refunded, so the real risk is £0."
- **"Let me think"** → "Totally — what's the one thing you're unsure about?" (answer the *real* objection) → "I only take 2 sprint clients a month, want me to hold your spot?"
- **"How do I know it works?"** → the refund + "I run my own and a mate's channel through the exact same system." *(Never invent clients — the refund carries it.)*
